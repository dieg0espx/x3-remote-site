# X3 Remote — Support

X3 Remote turns your Apple Watch into a shutter and mode selector for the Insta360 X3.

## Setup

1. Turn the X3 on. It must have been activated once in the official Insta360 app.
2. **iPhone connection:** open X3 Remote on the iPhone and tap **Connect to Camera**. Accept the Wi-Fi join and Local Network prompts. The phone can then stay in your pocket.
3. **Bluetooth connection (no phone needed):** on the watch, tap the status pill, choose **Bluetooth**, and tap **Reconnect**. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on the camera.
4. Press the big button to shoot. Swipe sideways to change modes. Red buttons start and stop recording.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Not connected" on the watch (iPhone mode):** open X3 Remote on the iPhone, tap Connect to Camera, then return to the watch.
- **iPhone cannot join the camera Wi-Fi:** check the camera's Wi-Fi is on, and that the network prefix and password in the iPhone app match your camera (default prefix `X3 `, default password `88888888`). You can also join the camera network manually in Settings ▸ Wi-Fi.
- **No internet on the iPhone while connected:** expected. The camera hotspot has no internet uplink. Disconnect to get internet back.
- **Watch shows "Timed out" but the photo was taken:** HDR photos can take several seconds. Keep the watch app in the foreground during capture.
- **Bluetooth mode says the camera rejected a command:** the camera is busy or that mode is not available on your firmware. Try again or switch to the iPhone connection.
- **A mode is missing:** the app only offers the modes your camera reports. Update the camera firmware with the Insta360 app.
