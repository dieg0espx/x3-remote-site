# X3 Remote — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 7, 2026**

X3 Remote ("the app") is an Apple Watch and iPhone remote control for the Insta360 X3 camera, published by Diego Espinosa.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** X3 Remote does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

## What stays on your devices

- **Camera Wi-Fi settings** (network name prefix and password) and the list of capture modes your camera reported are saved in the app's local storage on your iPhone so you do not have to enter them again. They never leave the device.
- **Your connection preference** (iPhone or Bluetooth) is saved locally on the watch.

## Network and Bluetooth access

- The iPhone app joins the camera's own Wi-Fi hotspot and sends commands to the camera at a local address. No traffic goes to the internet.
- The watch app can connect to the camera over Bluetooth. Bluetooth is used only to send shutter and mode commands to the camera and to read its status.
- The watch and the iPhone exchange commands and status through Apple's Watch Connectivity framework on your own devices.

## Permissions requested

- **Local Network** (iPhone): required by iOS to talk to the camera on its Wi-Fi hotspot.
- **Wi-Fi join** (iPhone): iOS asks before the app joins the camera's hotspot.
- **Bluetooth** (Apple Watch): required to control the camera directly from the watch.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
